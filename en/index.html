04 / SIMPLE WORKFLOW

From preparation to finishing

A practical look at how DETOSPA fits into a salon treatment.

01

Fill the shampoo basin with warm water

Submerge the intake strainer in the shampoo basin filled with warm water.

02

Start the device

Hold the micro/nanobubble outlet and press Start. Warm water circulates through the unit, where hydrogen dissolves into the water.

03

Approximately 10-minute head spa

Rinse the hair for approximately 10 minutes while massaging the scalp.

04

Blow-dry to finish

Blow-dry the hair thoroughly to finish.

FAQ

Does installation require special construction?

No special installation work is required. Check the local power supply and operating environment before use.

Is regular maintenance required?

Replace the purified water approximately every two weeks and clean the inside monthly with the dedicated maintenance solution. Follow the official manual.

Who is DETOSPA designed for?

DETOSPA is designed for professional salons looking to add a treatment option to their services.

What consumables are required?

As a guide, around two 500 mL bottles of purified water are used per month. The dedicated maintenance solution is sold separately.
